# Privacy policy

Last updated: <DATE>

This is a short, generic policy for a brand that uses weekpost to publish to its own Instagram professional account.
It is a starting point, not legal advice: read it, change it to match what you really do, and have it checked if your
situation needs it. Then publish it on a public web page and give that URL to Meta (see the README, step 4).

## Who we are

<BRAND> ("we") publishes posts on its own Instagram account using a tool called weekpost. You can reach us at
<CONTACT>.

## What the tool does

The tool prepares images, videos and captions that we wrote or approved, and publishes them on our own Instagram
professional account at the scheduled time. It does not publish on anyone else's behalf.

## What data we handle

- **Access token for our own account.** We store the token Instagram gives us to publish, in our own database, and
  renew it before it expires. We do not share it.
- **Post content and publishing status** (captions, media, times, errors), kept in our own database and repository.
- **Costs and logs** of the tool's runs, kept in our own accounts.

We do not use the tool to collect personal data about our followers, commenters or visitors, and we do not sell data.

## Services we use

The tool runs on services we have accounts with: Instagram (Meta), a database and file storage provider, a worker
hosting provider, GitHub, and, to make images and voice-overs, an AI model provider. Each processes data under its own
terms and privacy policy. Only the content of our posts is sent to the AI provider, never our token.

## Your rights

If you believe we hold personal data about you, write to <CONTACT>. We will tell you what we hold and delete it on
request, unless the law requires us to keep it.
