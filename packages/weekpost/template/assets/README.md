# assets

Placeholders so that `npx weekpost check` passes on a new project. Replace them with your own:

- `logo.svg`: your logo (the config names it under `brand.logo.file`).
- `fonts/`: Inter, under the SIL Open Font License (`OFL.txt`). Swap the files and `brand.font` in `weekpost.config.ts` for your brand font, and keep its license next to it.
- Music (optional) also goes here.
